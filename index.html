Keep your eyes on the game.

Score from your wrist on a Garmin or Wear OS watch, with a cheap Bluetooth remote clipped to the net, or with a tap on your earbuds. Start a game right on the watch, also basketball and football with a running clock.

Clubs and events, made easy.

For club training, tournaments and sports days. Players get a reminder in advance to confirm they’re coming. Skoor creates fair matches, matches players to available courts, and shows every score on the TV in the hall. At the end of the night, the statistics are already there.

  • Who is comingA reminder the day before; yes, no or maybe with one tap.
  • Fair rotationEveryone plays as much, with and against different people.
  • Live screensAny TV becomes a scoreboard: scan a code, done.
  • Rankings and seasonsPer evening, per season, per pair.
