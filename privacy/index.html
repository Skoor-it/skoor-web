Privacy

Privacy, in plain words

Skoor keeps as little as it can. No name unless you make an account, never your exact location, no advertising, and no tracking on this website.

Without an account

You don’t need an account to use Skoor. Open the app and score: your games stay on your phone, also offline, and Skoor doesn’t know who you are. Only while Help improve Skoor is on, each quick game is sent without any name (see below); switch it off and nothing leaves your phone. The watch apps, clubs and your results on all your devices need a free account.

Quick games

While Help improve Skoor is on (in the app: You → App), each quick game sends: the sport and its rules, the score and when each point was scored, the language and version of the app, your phone’s system (for example “iOS 18”) and its time zone. We also keep roughly where your internet connection is: the country, region and nearest town (for example “Belgium, Flanders, Ghent”), worked out from the connection’s internet address by our network provider. We never keep the address itself, your name or your account, and never your exact location. Switch it off and nothing more is sent.

Your account

Your name and email address, your clubs, and the matches and events you take part in. Your statistics are visible to your clubs; to others only if you choose so in your profile. You sign in with Google or with a link by email: we never see or keep a password.

Sport wishes

The sport you type, and roughly where your internet connection is (country, region and nearest town, as for quick games). Nothing else.

Mailing list

Not open yet. When it opens: only if you sign up, your email address, the topics you chose and your language. You confirm your address first, and every email has a link to stop at once.

This website

No cookies, no tracking and no advertising. The fonts come from our own server. The numbers on the home page (games being played and so on) are totals: they say nothing about you.

Who handles the data

Accounts, clubs and scores are stored by Google Firebase. The website and the app are served by Cloudflare. They only process the data for Skoor; we never sell it or share it for advertising.

Deleting and your rights

Write to hello@skoor.it to see, correct or delete what Skoor keeps about you; we answer within a month. Deleting your account removes your name, email and profile. Quick-game records without a name are deleted 24 months after the game.

Who we are

Skoor, made in Belgium. Questions about your data: hello@skoor.it. You may also complain to the Belgian Data Protection Authority (dataprotectionauthority.be).
