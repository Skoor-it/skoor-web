Terms of use

The rules, in plain words

Skoor keeps the score of your games and helps clubs run their evenings. By using the app, the watch apps, the TV screens or this website, you agree to these rules.

Free to use

Scoring games, keeping your results and organising who comes are free. Paid plans for extra features will come later; their price and what you get are always shown before you pay, and paying is never needed to keep your results.

Your account

Keep your sign-in to yourself. You are responsible for what is done with your account. Under 16? Ask a parent before you make an account.

What you put in

Scores, names, events and messages you add stay yours. You let Skoor store and show them to the people they are meant for (your club, the players, a TV screen at the venue). Only add other people’s names when they are fine with it.

Play fair

Don’t use Skoor to harass anyone, to post anything illegal or hurtful, or to break, overload or copy the service. We may remove such content and close an account that does this.

No guarantees

We do our best to keep Skoor working and your data safe, but it can have errors or be offline at times. Skoor is a help for keeping score, not an official referee: when it matters, the players or the official on court decide. As far as the law allows, Skoor is not liable for indirect damage or for lost scores.

Changes

Skoor keeps changing. When these rules change in a way that matters, we say so in the app before it applies. You can stop using Skoor at any time.

Law and contact

Belgian law applies. Questions or complaints: hello@skoor.it. How we handle your data is on the privacy page.
